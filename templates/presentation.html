Quantum Key Distribution - BB84 Protocol

The BB84 protocol ensures secure key exchange through quantum properties:

  1. Qubit Preparation: Alice encodes random bits in two quantum bases
  2. Qubit Transmission: Qubits are transmitted through quantum channel
  3. Measurement: Bob measures in randomly chosen bases
  4. Basis Reconciliation: They compare basis choices over classical channel
  5. Eavesdropping Detection: Error rate analysis reveals interception attempts
  6. Privacy Amplification: Final key generation with enhanced security
Key Advantage: Physically impossible to copy unknown quantum states without detection
