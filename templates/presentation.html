Quantum Key Distribution - BB84 Protocol

The BB84 protocol uses quantum properties to ensure secure key exchange:

  1. Alice sends qubits in random bases
  2. Bob measures in random bases
  3. They compare bases and keep matching measurements
  4. Check for eavesdropping with sample bits
  5. Apply privacy amplification for final key
